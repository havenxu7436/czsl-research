<h1 id="vg-cpod-方案-v1f-组实验与三卡实践命令">VG-CPOD 方案 v1：F 组实验与三卡实践命令</h1>
<p>日期：2026-10-20。框架：冻结 OpenAI CLIP ViT-L/14；数据：UT-Zappos；版本：v1。</p>
<p><strong>F1 = E1，固定 ρ=0.15，将 β 从 0.1 降到 0.05。</strong> 三个 seed 从各自原始初始化训练，直接与已有 E1 的相同 seed 比较。每项训练 10 epochs，随后自动完成 native／exact 两种选中权重的 TRAIN 均值门控评估。</p>
<h2 id="实验变量与三卡分工">1. 实验变量与三卡分工</h2>

<h3 id="β-对训练目标的精确作用">2.2 β 对训练目标的精确作用</h3>
<p>令 <math display="inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>𝒞</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>s</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>n</mi></mstyle></msub><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{seen}}</annotation></semantics></math> 为 TRAIN 组合集合，真实组合为 <math display="inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mi>a</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo><mo>∈</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>𝒞</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>s</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>n</mi></mstyle></msub></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">(a_b,o_b)\in\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{seen}}</annotation></semantics></math>。定义完整联合分布落在 TRAIN 组合上的总质量：</p>
<p><math display="block" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>Z</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>=</mo><munder><mo>∑</mo><mrow><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mi>a</mi><mi>i</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>j</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo><mo>∈</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>𝒞</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>s</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>n</mi></mstyle></msub></mrow></munder><mo>exp</mo><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mi>s</mi><mrow><mi>b</mi><mi>j</mi><mi>i</mi></mrow></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo><mo>,</mo><mspace width="2.0em"></mspace><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>s</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>p</mi><mi>p</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>t</mi></mstyle></msub><mo>=</mo><mo>−</mo><mfrac><mn>1</mn><mi>B</mi></mfrac><munderover><mo>∑</mo><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><mi>B</mi></munderover><mo>log</mo><msub><mi>Z</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mi>.</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">
Z_b=\sum_{(a_i,o_j)\in\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{seen}}}\exp(s_{bji}),
\qquad \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{support}}=-\frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\log Z_b.
</annotation></semantics></math></p>
<p>它是组合集合的概率质量。组合分类损失在 TRAIN 组合内归一化：</p>
<p><math display="block" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mtable><mtr><mtd columnalign="right"><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>p</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>r</mi></mstyle></msub></mtd><mtd columnalign="left"><mo>=</mo><mfrac><mn>1</mn><mi>B</mi></mfrac><munderover><mo>∑</mo><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><mi>B</mi></munderover><mrow><mo stretchy="true" form="prefix">[</mo><mo>−</mo><msub><mi>s</mi><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>a</mi><mi>b</mi></msub></mrow></msub><mo>+</mo><mo>log</mo><msub><mi>Z</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo stretchy="true" form="postfix">]</mo></mrow><mo>,</mo></mtd></mtr><mtr><mtd columnalign="right"><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mi>a</mi></msub></mtd><mtd columnalign="left"><mo>=</mo><mo>−</mo><mfrac><mn>1</mn><mi>B</mi></mfrac><munderover><mo>∑</mo><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><mi>B</mi></munderover><mo>log</mo><mi>p</mi><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mi>a</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>∣</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo><mo>,</mo></mtd></mtr><mtr><mtd columnalign="right"><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mi>o</mi></msub></mtd><mtd columnalign="left"><mo>=</mo><mo>−</mo><mfrac><mn>1</mn><mi>B</mi></mfrac><munderover><mo>∑</mo><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><mi>B</mi></munderover><mo>log</mo><mi>p</mi><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>∣</mo><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo><mi>.</mi></mtd></mtr></mtable><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}}&amp;=\frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}
\left[-s_{b,o_b,a_b}+\log Z_b\right],\\
\mathcal{L}_a&amp;=-\frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\log p(a_b\mid o_b,x_b),\\
\mathcal{L}_o&amp;=-\frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\log p(o_b\mid x_b).
\end{aligned}
</annotation></semantics></math></p>
<p>由 <math display="inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mi>s</mi><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>a</mi><mi>b</mi></msub></mrow></msub><mo>=</mo><mo>log</mo><mi>p</mi><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>∣</mo><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo><mo>+</mo><mo>log</mo><mi>p</mi><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mi>a</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>∣</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>x</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">s_{b,o_b,a_b}=\log p(o_b\mid x_b)+\log p(a_b\mid o_b,x_b)</annotation></semantics></math>，逐样本相加并取平均可得：</p>
<p><math display="block" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mtable><mtr><mtd columnalign="right"><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mi>a</mi></msub><mo>+</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mi>o</mi></msub></mtd><mtd columnalign="left"><mo>=</mo><mo>−</mo><mfrac><mn>1</mn><mi>B</mi></mfrac><munder><mo>∑</mo><mi>b</mi></munder><msub><mi>s</mi><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>o</mi><mi>b</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><msub><mi>a</mi><mi>b</mi></msub></mrow></msub></mtd></mtr><mtr><mtd columnalign="right"></mtd><mtd columnalign="left"><mo>=</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>p</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>r</mi></mstyle></msub><mo>+</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>s</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>p</mi><mi>p</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>t</mi></mstyle></msub><mo>,</mo></mtd></mtr><mtr><mtd columnalign="right"><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>t</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>k</mi></mstyle></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><mi>β</mi><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo></mtd><mtd columnalign="left"><mo>=</mo><mfrac><mn>1.5</mn><mrow><mn>1</mn><mo>+</mo><mi>β</mi></mrow></mfrac><mrow><mo stretchy="true" form="prefix">[</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>p</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>r</mi></mstyle></msub><mo>+</mo><mi>β</mi><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">(</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mi>a</mi></msub><mo>+</mo><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mi>o</mi></msub><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">)</mo><mo stretchy="true" form="postfix">]</mo></mrow></mtd></mtr><mtr><mtd columnalign="right"></mtd><mtd columnalign="left"><mo>=</mo><mn>1.5</mn><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>p</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>r</mi></mstyle></msub><mo>+</mo><mfrac><mrow><mn>1.5</mn><mi>β</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>1</mn><mo>+</mo><mi>β</mi></mrow></mfrac><msub><mstyle mathvariant="script"><mi>ℒ</mi></mstyle><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>s</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>p</mi><mi>p</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>t</mi></mstyle></msub><mi>.</mi></mtd></mtr></mtable><annotation \begin{aligned}
\mathcal{L}_a+\mathcal{L}_o
&amp;=-\frac{1}{B}\sum_b s_{b,o_b,a_b}\\
&amp;=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{support}},\\
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{task}}(\beta)
&amp;=\frac{1.5}{1+\beta}\left[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}}+\beta(\mathcal{L}_a+\mathcal{L}_o)\right]\\
&amp;=1.5\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}}+\frac{1.5\beta}{1+\beta}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{support}}.
\end{aligned}
</annotation></semantics></math></p>

<h3 id="权重选择与六项均值门控评估">2.3 权重选择与六项均值门控评估</h3>
<p>对第 <math display="inline" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mi>t</mi><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">t</annotation></semantics></math> 轮的 VAL CW native／exact AUC，分别选择首次最大值：</p>
<p><math display="block" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msubsup><mi>t</mi><mi>m</mi><mo>*</mo></msubsup><mo>=</mo><mo>min</mo><mrow><mo stretchy="true" form="prefix">{</mo><mi>t</mi><mo>∈</mo><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">{</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>,</mo><mi>…</mi><mo>,</mo><mi>T</mi><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">}</mo><mo>:</mo><msubsup><mi>A</mi><mi>t</mi><mi>m</mi></msubsup><mo>=</mo><munder><mo>max</mo><mrow><mn>1</mn><mo>≤</mo><mi>s</mi><mo>≤</mo><mi>T</mi></mrow></munder><msubsup><mi>A</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>m</mi></msubsup><mo stretchy="true" form="postfix">}</mo></mrow><mo>,</mo><mspace width="2.0em"></mspace><mi>m</mi><mo>∈</mo><mo stretchy="false" form="prefix">{</mo><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>n</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>t</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>v</mi><mi>e</mi></mstyle><mo>,</mo><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>e</mi><mi>x</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>c</mi><mi>t</mi></mstyle><mo stretchy="false" form="postfix">}</mo><mi>.</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">

</annotation></semantics></math></p>
<p>两种最优值分别维护，只有严格增大才更新。保存 <code>best_native.pt</code>、其逐字别名 <code>best.pt</code>、<code>best_exact.pt</code> 和包含优化器／随机状态的 <code>last.pt</code>。主结果在运行根目录；exact 选中结果在 <code>exact_selection/</code>。</p>
<p>每个选中 checkpoint 用全部 TRAIN 图像统计物体／模式均值，并固定该权重替换推理时的门控：</p>
<p><math display="block" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msub><mover><mi>γ</mi><mo accent="true">‾</mo></mover><mrow><mi>j</mi><mi>k</mi></mrow></msub><mo>=</mo><mfrac><mn>1</mn><msub><mi>N</mi><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>t</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>n</mi></mstyle></msub></mfrac><munderover><mo>∑</mo><mrow><mi>b</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>1</mn></mrow><msub><mi>N</mi><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>t</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>n</mi></mstyle></msub></munderover><msub><mi>γ</mi><mrow><mi>b</mi><mi>j</mi><mi>k</mi></mrow></msub><mo>,</mo><mspace width="2.0em"></mspace><msubsup><mi>γ</mi><mrow><mi>b</mi><mi>j</mi><mi>k</mi></mrow><mstyle mathvariant="normal"><mi>m</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>n</mi></mstyle></msubsup><mo>=</mo><msub><mover><mi>γ</mi><mo accent="true">‾</mo></mover><mrow><mi>j</mi><mi>k</mi></mrow></msub><mi>.</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">

<p>统计不使用标签。native／exact 各三项，共六项评估，优化步数为零。每种权重、每种门控条件分别使用自己的 VAL CW／OW native bias，再固定到 TEST；同时报告 native 与 exact AUC。</p>
